# Find out how asqr sessions could be answered from a phone

Jakob wants to look into asqr sessions from his phone (chat, 2026-11-05).
While he is away from the console, an agent can only reach him by push
notification, and the asqr session it asked waits until he is back at
the terminal. Being able to read and answer a session on the phone would
let agents' work continue in that time.

## What to find out

- Which way would work: a phone app, a web view, or something else, and
  what asqr would need for it.
- How a session gets from the machine to the phone. Jakob expects this
  to need network-based sessions in some form, a different procedure
  from today's local queue.
- What of the session format and its context types (images, ANSI,
  diffs, Markdown) a phone can show.

Jakob does not know yet how it would work and wants to look at it
later; nothing is decided.
